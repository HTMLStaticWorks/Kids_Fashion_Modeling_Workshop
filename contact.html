Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need To Know

Got questions about enrollment, age groups, or workshop schedules? Here are answers to common queries.

What age groups are eligible to join?

We offer tailored groups for kids aged 4 to 16+ years old, categorized by age and confidence level so every child feels comfortable.

Do children need prior modeling experience?

Not at all! Our workshops are designed for complete beginners as well as children looking to refine their stage presence and public speaking.

What are the batch sizes and schedules?

We keep batch sizes small (8–12 kids) to ensure personalized attention. Sessions run on weekday evenings and weekend morning/afternoon slots.

Can parents watch the training sessions?

Parents are welcome to attend showcase days and designated observation sessions to watch their child's progress firsthand.

What should kids wear to the workshop?

Comfortable casual attire or smart clothing with flat shoes or sneakers. Specific wardrobe guidelines are provided prior to showcase events.

How do I confirm enrollment for my child?

Fill out the enquiry form above or reach out via phone/WhatsApp. Our studio coordinator will contact you to confirm batch availability.
